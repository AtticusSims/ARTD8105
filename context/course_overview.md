## What the course is

ARTD8105 Advanced Topics in Digital Art. Doctoral, Semester 1 2026/27, fourteen numbered sessions, Wednesdays 10:00–13:00. Two outcomes by 25 November: **a made thing**, shown in the Faculty group exhibition, and **a submission-ready research paper** aimed at a venue the student selects and studies themselves.

The paper is not a report about the project. It argues what is now known that was not known before, based on what making the thing revealed.

## The spine

Sessions are ordered by where the material of a work originates, which is also a ladder of decreasing authorial control:

**rule in 2D → rule in 3D → external data → live input → captured reality → learned models**

At the top the author controls everything. At the bottom they are choosing, framing and constraining rather than specifying. Each rung is a different answer to what a designer's authorship consists of. Fabrication runs across this as an output axis from session 3.

## The four graded moments

23 September — first fabrication output · 7 October — idea evaluation defended · 11 November — presentation, peer review, oral authorship check · 25 November — submission package and final critique.

## The line on AI

**Code and making:** the student directs, the agent implements. Assessed on decisions, debugging and the ability to explain the result — not on having typed it.

**The paper:** the student writes. The claim, the interpretation, the argument and the prose are theirs. The agent finds, checks, critiques and corrects grammar. It never writes the argument, and never the critical-thinking section of a reading note.

## Tools

Cursor throughout. p5.js and browser-based vector output for 2D. Blender with Geometry Nodes and `bpy` for procedural 3D. MediaPipe and the Faculty's brain–computer interface devices for live input. Photogrammetry, structured-light scanning and three.js for capture. Teachable Machine and ml5.js for trained models as material. Fabrication on the Faculty laser cutter, large-format printer, embroidery machine and 3D printers.

No new tool after session 9. The final five sessions are for making and writing.
