|---|---|

Each contribution must contain three components: **the problem you address**,
**your novel method**, and **the benefit or advantage**.

Do not just describe what you did — present what is gained by doing it. You are
convincing a reviewer that you addressed an important problem with a novel
method that has clear advantages.
