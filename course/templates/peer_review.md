## Required: the weakest claim

**Name the single weakest claim in the paper. Not the weakest section — the
weakest claim. Quote it, and say why it does not hold.**

> Quoted claim:

> Why it does not hold:

> What would be needed to make it hold:

*This is compulsory and it is the most useful thing you will write. Reviewers
default to safe generalities. One specific, actionable objection is worth more
to the author than a page of balanced observation.*

---

## Contribution check

- **What do you understand their contribution to be?** State it in one sentence,
  in your own words, without describing their artifact.

- **Does it match what they say it is?** If not, that mismatch is the single
  most valuable thing you can report.

- **Is it contestable?** Could a reasonable person disagree? If nothing could
  falsify it, it is not a claim.

---

## Method account

- Can you tell **how they worked** and **how they analysed what they recorded**
  to reach their finding?
- Could you follow their process from this description?
- *(This section is where amateur papers give themselves away. Be honest.)*

## Evidence

- Is every claim traceable to documentation, journal or artifact?
- Any claim resting on assertion or memory?
- Any citation you would want to check yourself? **Say which** — you are not
  obliged to check, but flagging is a real service.

## Notes for reviewers

**Reviewing is the strongest learning moment in this course.** You will
understand your own paper better after attacking someone else's than after any
amount of advice about academic writing. Take it seriously.

**Be hard and be specific.** Vague encouragement wastes your peer's time. The
kindest review is the one that identifies the real problem while there is still
time to fix it.

**You may use the agent to help you review — but not to write the review.**
Useful:

```
@venue/<their-slug>.md
I'm reviewing a peer's draft against their venue's criteria. For each
criterion, tell me what evidence in a draft would satisfy it, so I know what
to look for. Then I'll write my assessment and you tell me where I'm being
vague or unfair.
```

**You are also validating their venue file.** If you cannot apply their derived
standard because it is too vague to act on, say so in your review. That is a
finding about their Step 4 work and it is worth more to them than politeness.
