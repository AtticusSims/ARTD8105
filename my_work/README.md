# my_work

**This folder is yours.** Nothing here is ever touched by `git pull course main`.

Everything you make for this course goes in here: your instruction, the tools you build, your prints, your reading notes, your process journal, and eventually your paper.

## The folders

| | |
|---|---|
| `instruction.md` | Your instruction, current version. Keep the earlier versions as separate files. |
| `parameters.md` | Every decision your instruction leaves open — the phrase it came from, its range, your chosen value, and why. |
| `tools/` | Generators you build. One file each, self-contained. |
| `prints/` | Exhibition output. SVGs plus `labels.txt`. |
| `reading_notes/` | One file per paper, named `author_year.md` — for example `frayling1993.md`. The naming matters: in session 11 you will point the agent at this whole folder. |
| `process_journal/` | One entry per event, not per day. Named by date. |
| `venue/` | Venue files, from session 6. One per venue. |
| `paper/` | The manuscript. |
| `_local/` | Anything large that is not evidence. **Git ignores this folder entirely** — it stays on your computer. |

## Templates

Copy them out of `course/templates/` into the right folder here before filling them in. Never fill one in where it sits.

## What goes in `_local/`

Reading PDFs, video, raw camera files, Blender working files, photogrammetry image sets — anything big that is working material rather than evidence.

Photographs of your physical work, screenshots that show a finding, and your SVG and HTML output are **evidence** and belong in the folders above, committed like everything else.
